Resolving Double-Clicking & Switch Chatter Problems

Is my mouse double clicking when I only click once?

If files open unexpectedly when single-clicked, highlighted text drops mid-drag, or your web browser registers two clicks instead of one, your switch is experiencing mechanical chatter.

How do I know if my mouse is double clicking?

Use our Double-Click Switch Chatter Test module above. Click your primary button slowly several times. If multiple click events register within an interval under 80ms from a single press, your device has a confirmed hardware chatter fault.

Why is my double-click not working or double-firing randomly?

This happens when the internal microswitch metal leaf springs lose tension or accumulate micro-oxidation. When pressed, the contacts bounce rapidly, confusing the operating system into reading multiple clicks.

How to fix a double-clicking mouse

  • Adjust Debounce Delay: Increase debounce filtering time in companion software (like Logitech G Hub or Razer Synapse) to 10ms–15ms.
  • Discharge Static Charge: Unplug your mouse or remove batteries, then hold down all buttons for 30 seconds to clear residual capacitance.
  • Clear Debris: Spray compressed air around the plastic button plungers to clear dust causing contact friction.

Troubleshooting Inconsistent Left & Right Clicks

Why is my left click not consistent?

An inconsistent left click—where dragging files feels sluggish or game actions fail to register—is often caused by a worn plastic plunger indentation wearing down inside the shell, resulting in uneven force transmission to the internal switch.

Why is my right click not consistent?

Because the secondary button is used less frequently, intermittent right clicks usually point toward background software hooks, custom macro conflicts, or minor grime buildup beneath the shell ridge.

Fixing Scroll Wheel Glitches & Ghost Scrolling

Why is my scroll wheel not clicking?

The middle mouse button relies on a separate tact switch underneath the wheel axle. If the wheel assembly shifts laterally or dust blocks the plunger stem, downward depression will fail.

Why is my mouse wheel stuck or mouse scroll is scrolling on its own?

If scrolling down causes pages to jump upward erratically (ghost scrolling), or if the wheel is physically jammed, lint and hair have likely wrapped around the optical/mechanical encoder wheel axle. Clear the obstruction with compressed air while rotating the wheel rapidly.
